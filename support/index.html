Playing a puzzle

Grow from any connected crystal square. A plus sign marks an available move. Connect every gem before your moves run out. Matching corner marks permit diagonal growth; a lever opens its matching gate. Rails allow travel in the direction shown.

Hint highlights a short route on the board. Undo takes back one move for 1 gem. Restart keeps your completed progress and starts the current board again. Tools show their gem cost before use. Buying an extra move costs 3 gems.

Keeping your progress

Progress, tools, gems and records are saved on the installed device. Install updates over the existing app. Do not delete the app as a troubleshooting step.

Transfer progress can copy campaign progress and personal times. It does not transfer the gem wallet, purchased gem balance, gear or bonus records. There is no Crystal Branch account or automatic game cloud sync. Deleting the app, clearing its data or changing phones may lose local records and gems.

Replay and practice let you revisit puzzles. Replaying an already rewarded clear or bonus does not earn its campaign reward again.

Purchases and Restore Purchases

The game is free. Optional gem packs and the monthly Remove Ads subscription are available from the start in Purchases & subscriptions. Apple displays the current price and asks you to confirm.

Remove Ads removes automatic ad breaks while the subscription is active. It does not include tools or a gem allowance. It renews automatically unless canceled through Apple. Manage or cancel an Apple subscription.

Restore Purchases checks your ad-free entitlement using your Apple Account. Consumable gem packs are not restored as a fresh gem credit; Restore Purchases should not increase your wallet. Keep the app installed if a gem delivery looks wrong and contact support with the product and approximate purchase time. Apple handles billing and refund requests.

In TestFlight, Apple purchases use the sandbox and do not charge real money. Test subscriptions can renew and expire faster than real subscriptions.

Ads and privacy choices

Automatic ad breaks occur at breaks in play when an ad is available. A gap between ads is not the length of an ad. The ad service controls the creative and when a close button becomes available. If no ad is ready, the game should let you continue.

Ad privacy choices appears in Privacy & help when Google's consent service requires it. An active Remove Ads entitlement suppresses automatic ads. Report an ad problem by email with your app version and what appeared; do not repeatedly tap ads to test them.

Sound or a stuck screen

Check the game's music and effects settings and your iPhone volume. If the screen gets stuck, first close and reopen the app. Keep the app installed and tell us the last button you tapped. Do not erase your saves.
