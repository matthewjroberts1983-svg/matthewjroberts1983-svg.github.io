Game data on your device

The game stores puzzle progress, solutions, personal times, gem balance, tools, bonus records, sound settings and purchase-delivery records locally. The installed app also keeps a local native checkpoint to protect supported updates and interrupted gem delivery. That checkpoint is not an online backup.

There is no Crystal Branch login, developer-operated progress server or separate game analytics service. The game does not automatically send your puzzle history to the developer. Local diagnostic records can include moves, restarts, timing and ad events. If you choose to export and send a report, it contains the records shown in that export.

Transfer codes contain campaign progress and personal times, not the gem wallet. Share a code or report only when you intend to share those records. Reinstalling, clearing data or changing phones can lose local saves and consumable gems.

Apple purchases

Apple processes payments and subscriptions. The app uses Apple's StoreKit service to verify products, transactions and the current ad-free entitlement. It records transaction identifiers and delivery information locally to prevent duplicate gem credits. We do not receive your full payment-card details from the game.

Remove Ads is a monthly subscription that removes automatic ads while active. Gem packs are consumable. Restore Purchases checks eligible ad-free ownership; it does not reissue consumable gems. Apple's privacy policy explains Apple's processing.

Google advertising and consent

The public release is being prepared to use Google AdMob. Google's advertising and consent services can process device or app identifiers, an IP address and approximate location derived from it, ad views and interactions, other product interactions, crash information and performance diagnostics. This information can support ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and service operations. The game does not request access to your precise location.

Personalized ads are optional. For the planned public release, Crystal Branch will request them only when both your Apple tracking permission and Google privacy choices allow them. With both permissions, Google and its advertising partners may use device identifiers and activity across apps and websites to personalize ads and measure their performance. All 300 puzzles stay free with either choice.

If you decline, you may still see Limited Ads. These do not use personal data to personalize ads, but Google may still process information such as your IP address to deliver ads and prevent fraud. Programmatic Limited Ads may also use cookies and local storage for fraud detection.

You can review Google's choices under Settings → Privacy & help → Ad privacy choices when available. Manage Apple tracking permission in your device's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Declining permissions does not block gameplay. If a privacy request fails or an ad is unavailable, the game remains playable.

An active Remove Ads subscription stops automatic ads. Privacy and consent information may still be updated while it is active. The subscription does not erase information already processed by an ad service.

See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps for their practices and controls.

Support and reports you send

If you email us, we receive your address and whatever you include, such as a screenshot, diagnostic export or description of the problem. Diagnostic and recovery exports can include puzzle history, times, gem and tool records, purchase-delivery identifiers and ad-decision traces. Share only what is needed for your request. We use that information to answer, troubleshoot and handle your request. Our email provider processes the message for delivery and storage. Do not send passwords, verification codes, full payment-card details or unnecessary personal information.

Support messages are kept as needed to handle the request, resolve a dispute or meet applicable obligations. You may ask us to delete support information we hold, subject to information we need to retain for those purposes.

This support website

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It includes no added advertising, analytics scripts, account forms or embedded tracking pixels. The hosting provider processes web requests; GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub's privacy statement. Following an external link uses that provider's service.

Beta testing

TestFlight uses sample ads and Apple's sandbox purchases. Apple separately processes beta usage, crash and feedback information and shares testing information with the developer. See Apple's TestFlight privacy information.

Children and families

Crystal Branch is a general-public, family-friendly puzzle game. It is not specifically designed or marketed for children under 13. The purchase area includes a parent check, which is not age verification or permission to track a child. The game does not ask players for a name, email address or date of birth. A parent or guardian with a concern can contact us at the address above.

Your controls and requests

You can use the game's privacy choices when available, manage subscriptions through Apple and contact us about information you sent to support. We do not have a developer server from which to retrieve or erase your local puzzle history or wallet. Erasing app data can permanently remove that local information; it does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion, or to limit certain uses. Email us to make a request. We may need reasonable information to verify it. We cannot promise to retrieve or erase information held independently by Apple, Google or your hosting provider; their own controls and policies also apply.

Changes

We will update this page when the game's data practices change. The date above identifies the latest policy version.
