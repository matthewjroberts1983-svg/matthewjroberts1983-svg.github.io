A little deeper. One more discovery.

Find your way through the mine.

Connect the gems, plan your route and see what waits around the next bend.

Explore 500 free puzzles through mines and caverns in Crystal Branch: Path Puzzles, a game by Matthew Roberts. Plan a route to connect the gems before your moves run out. Along the way, catch gems in the Gem Cart and aim for the Timber Bullseye. Gem packs and the monthly Remove Ads subscription are optional.

Available on the U.S. App Store for iPhone and iPad.
